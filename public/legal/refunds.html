Refunds

Last updated: 25 August 2026

The policy

Fourteen days, no questions asked, on any plan. Email support@mindsharp.app with the address you bought with. You do not need to explain yourself.

After fourteen days: if MindSharp was broken, or it did not do what the paywall said it would, tell us and we will refund it. If you simply stopped using it, cancel instead — you keep access to the end of the period you have already paid for.

Cancelling

Open MindSharp, tap the Pro badge, and choose Manage subscription. That opens the Lemon Squeezy customer portal, where you can cancel, change your card, or download invoices.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund. It stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the period you paid for runs out — we do not take access away the moment you cancel, because you already paid for it.

What happens to your data

A refund or an expiry ends Pro access. It does not delete your account or your answer history — if you come back, your streak and your weak-spot profile are still there. If you want it all deleted, ask, and we will.

Lifetime purchases

Covered by the same fourteen days. After that, "lifetime" means for as long as MindSharp runs. If we ever shut it down we will give 30 days' notice; see the Terms.

Who processes the refund

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record, so the refund is issued by them and appears on your statement from them. We authorise it; they move the money. It usually lands within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Chargebacks

Please email us before raising a chargeback. A chargeback costs us considerably more than the subscription is worth and takes months to resolve. We would much rather just refund you.
