Privacy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

The short version

Without an account, nothing about you leaves your browser. With an account, we store your email address and your answer history — because the weak-spot report is built from it and there is no way to give you that feature without keeping it. We do not sell data, we do not run third-party advertising trackers, and analytics is cookieless and aggregate.

What we collect

If you never sign in

Your scores, streaks and settings are written to your browser's local storage and stay there. We never receive them. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently — there is no copy to restore from.

If you sign in

  • Your email address — to sign you in and to attach a purchase to your account.
  • Your runs — game, difficulty, score, counts, duration, and when it happened.
  • Your attempts — one row per problem you answer: the operation, the two numbers, the correct answer, what you entered, whether it was right, and how long you took. This is what the weak-spot report and Drill mode read. It is the whole product; if you do not want it stored, use MindSharp without an account.
  • Your entitlement — which plan you are on, its status, and when the current period ends.

Payments

We never see your card. Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and handles payment entirely. We receive a customer id, an order or subscription id, and the email used at checkout — enough to know what you bought and to attach it to your account. Nothing more.

Analytics

We use privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics that records page views and product events (a game started, a paywall viewed) in aggregate. No cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, no profile of you as a person. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Logs

Our host keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses, for a short period. We use IP addresses for rate limiting — specifically to stop someone brute-forcing licence keys — and for nothing else.

Why we are allowed to hold it

Under the GDPR: performing our contract with you (your account, your purchase, the features you paid for) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and un-abused (rate limiting, fraud prevention).

How long we keep it

Your attempt history is kept while your account exists, because the report is built from it. Delete your account and it goes with it immediately. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires — that obligation sits with the merchant of record.

Deleting your account

Open Your account and choose Delete my account. It happens straight away, not on a queue: your profile, entitlement, runs, attempts, XP, streak, achievements and league standing are removed, and there is no copy to restore from. You do not need to email anyone and we will not try to talk you out of it.

Two things survive, and only these. Payment records held by our merchant of record, or by Apple or Google, are kept for as long as tax law requires. Our own log of the payment notifications they sent us is kept so a refund can still be honoured after the account is gone.

If you bought a subscription through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it there first. We cannot cancel it for you — only the store can — and deleting your account would otherwise leave your card being charged for something you no longer have. A subscription bought on the web is cancelled for you automatically as part of the deletion.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to processing. Deletion is in the app, immediately, as above. For anything else email support@mindsharp.app and we will action it within 30 days. Anonymous aggregates that cannot be traced back to you may remain.

Who else touches it

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Vercel — hosting.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments made on the web, as merchant of record.
  • Apple and Google — payments made inside the App Store and Play Store apps. Their own billing is mandatory there; we never see your card, and we only ask them whether a purchase is genuine.

Supabase, Vercel and Lemon Squeezy are processors acting on our instructions, and none of them may use your data for their own purposes. Apple and Google act as their own controllers for a store purchase — their privacy policies govern what they do with it, not ours.

Children

MindSharp is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
