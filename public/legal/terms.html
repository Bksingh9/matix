1. What MindSharp is

MindSharp is a timed mental-arithmetic practice tool. It measures how quickly and accurately you do arithmetic, and builds practice sets from your own answer history.

What we do not claim. MindSharp will not make you smarter, raise your IQ, prevent cognitive decline, or improve your performance at anything other than arithmetic. The scientific evidence that brain-training transfers to general cognitive ability is contested. What we measure, and the only thing we claim, is that you get faster and more accurate at the arithmetic you practise here.

2. Accounts

You can play without an account. An account is required to buy Pro, to sync progress across devices, and to use the weak-spot report and Drill mode, because those read your answer history.

We sign you in with a one-time link sent to your email address. Keep access to that address secure; anyone who can read it can sign in as you.

You must be old enough to consent to data processing where you live, or have a parent or guardian's permission. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.
